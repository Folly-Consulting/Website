Folly Consulting Corp. is based in Airdrie, Alberta and was incorporated in {{ site.data.company.incorporated }}. We advise on, design and deliver network and audio-visual systems, and build the tools and automation that support them.

Lucas Harrison, Lead Consultant

Lucas's career in technical support and client service began in 2009. It has taken him through technical support at Shaw Direct, years at Apple as a Genius and trainer, and work as an Apple Authorised Service Technician.

That background shows in how Folly works: managing portfolios of clients with very different needs, writing service strategies and proposals around their business, building custom deployment packages for managed devices, developing internal tools to track equipment, and documenting everything so it can be maintained and handed over. He also brings experience coordinating events, schedules and budgets with many stakeholders, which carries into special projects.

His certifications include Dante Certification Level 2, Fortinet NSE 3 and Apple Certified Support Professional.

Our mark is a pine ridge on a cliff edge: steady ground and a clear view. It reflects how we work: practical, honest and built to last.
