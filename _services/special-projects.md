Some jobs do not fit a category. They may be one-off, unusual, urgent, or simply unclear at the start. We take on special projects by first working out what is actually being asked, checking that it is feasible, and then owning it through to a result.

That can include proofs of concept, relocations and build-outs, temporary setups, stubborn troubleshooting, and audits or second opinions on work someone else has done.
