Networks, AV and other systems often come from different vendors and do not talk to each other. Integration is the work of making them behave as one: designing the network behind AV, simplifying control, and connecting the platforms your people actually use.

We test each part, document how it fits together, and hand it over so that it can be supported. The aim is a system that is simple for the people using it, even when it is not simple underneath.
